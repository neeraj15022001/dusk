# Code of Conduct

Dusk welcomes people of every background and experience level. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, including interactions by people representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Accept corrections, respect boundaries, and help newcomers provide useful reports. Discuss AI-assisted work on its quality, provenance, and evidence rather than assumptions about its author.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are abusive automation or repeated unsolicited promotional posts.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for abusive content, harassment, or privacy violations. For routine moderation, you may tag [@neeraj15022001](https://github.com/neeraj15022001) in the affected thread when it is safe to do so. Do not publish sensitive evidence or personal information in an issue. Reports involving the maintainer should be sent directly to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants, or block participation based on severity and repeated behavior. Serious threats or disclosure of private information can lead to immediate removal. Moderation should be proportionate, consistent, and respectful of reporter privacy. Appeals may be raised through available maintainer contact options on GitHub or GitHub Support for platform-level action.

This is a project-specific policy; it does not replace GitHub's terms and community guidelines.
