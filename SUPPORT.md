# Getting help

Start with the [README](README.md), [hardware and testing notes](docs/TESTING.md), and existing [issues](https://github.com/neeraj15022001/dusk/issues).

For a reproducible problem, use the bug report form. For a new behavior, use the feature request form. Include the Dusk version or commit, macOS version, MacBook model/chip, and selected effect. Do not share serial numbers or unredacted logs.

If your sensor is unavailable, the in-app angle simulator and four-second preview still work. Unsupported sensors leave the desktop clear. Quit other hinge utilities and retry if the app reports that the sensor could not open.

To clear an effect, reopen the lid or press **Control–Option–Command–K**. Escape pauses Dusk when settings has keyboard focus. You can also pause or quit from Dusk's menu bar icon. Closing the settings window does not quit the app.

For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns, follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Support is community-based and provided when maintainers are available.
