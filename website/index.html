A few things to know.

Will it work on my MacBook?+

Automatic effects need a compatible Apple HID hinge angle sensor. Development checks covered an M3 MacBook Air; support varies by model. Unsupported sensors leave your desktop clear, and you can still use the app’s simulator and four-second preview. See compatibility notes.

Does the website read my actual hinge angle?+

No. This is a synthetic Three.js demonstration controlled by your mouse, touch, or keyboard. The native Dusk app reads the hinge sensor locally. The browser preview approximates the effects; macOS controls the native backdrop blur.

What about accessibility and normal sleep?+

Dusk uses Fade when Reduce Motion is enabled. It keeps normal macOS sleep behavior and clears effects on pause, quit, sleep, and stale sensor readings. The overlay is a visual effect, not a lock screen or a privacy boundary.

Can I contribute, including with AI tools?+

Yes. Dusk was built with substantial Codex assistance under the maintainer’s direction. Focused patches, hardware reports, and accessibility improvements are welcome. Read the contribution guide and AI policy.
